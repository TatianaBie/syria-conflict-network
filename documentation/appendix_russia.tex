\subsection{Coding Russian involvement, 2015--2016}
\label{app:russia}

Although the Russian air campaign in Syria began on 30 September 2015, UCDP
GED lists Russia as a party to only one event in our extract, an event in the
conflict between the Government of Russia and the Caucasus Emirate. Russian
air support to the Military Forces of Syria is otherwise not recorded in the
actor columns. We recover it by combining two independent signals, textual
evidence in the UCDP source fields and proximity to Russian strikes documented
by Airwars, and add Russia as an actor only when both agree.
Table~\ref{tab:russia} summarises each step for 2015 and 2016.

\paragraph{Textual evidence.}
We search the source fields of every UCDP event, that is, the cited articles,
their headlines and the original sources, for references to Russia, such as ``Russian warplanes'', ``Russian airstrikes'' or ``Russian
forces''. Event $i$ receives $R_i=1$ if at least one field mentions Russia,
and $R_i=0$ otherwise. The flag is based on the text alone; the date and
location of an event play no role at this stage.

\paragraph{Airwars strikes.}
Airwars documents incidents of alleged civilian harm from military action and
records the belligerents to which each incident is attributed. We collect all
$1{,}811$ incidents in Syria attributed to the Russian military between
30 September 2015 and 31 December 2016 ($336$ in 2015 and $1{,}475$ in
2016).\footnote{Airwars, \emph{Russian Military in Syria} incident archive,
\url{https://airwars.org}, accessed October 2026.} Each incident has a date and
coordinates, which for most incidents are precise only to the level of a
village, neighbourhood or town.

For UCDP event $i$ with start date $t_i$ and Airwars incident $k$ with date
$t_k$, we define the match indicator
\begin{equation}
  M_i(\Delta_t,\Delta_d) \;=\; \mathbf{1}\bigl\{\exists\, k \in \mathcal{A}:
  \; |t_i - t_k| \le \Delta_t \;\text{ and }\; d_{ik} \le \Delta_d \bigr\},
  \label{eq:russia_match}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{A}$ is the set of Airwars incidents, $\Delta_t$ is measured in
days and $d_{ik}$ is the great-circle distance between the two locations in
kilometres. One qualifying incident is sufficient. We use a \emph{strict}
match, $M_i(0,5)$, which requires an Airwars incident on the same day within
$5$~km, and a \emph{broad} match, $M_i(1,15)$, which allows a difference of
one day and a distance of up to $15$~km. The broad window accounts for the
coarse geolocation of Airwars incidents and for reports that date an attack to
the following day. Every strict match is also a broad match.

\paragraph{Assignment rule.}
Russia is added as an actor to event $i$ if and only if
\begin{equation}
  R_i \cdot M_i(1,15) \;=\; 1,
  \label{eq:russia_assign}
\end{equation}
that is, if the event's own sources mention Russia and a Russian strike is
documented nearby on the same or an adjacent day. Neither signal suffices on
its own: a textual mention may refer to Russia only in passing, and a nearby
strike may be unrelated to the reported event. Russia is entered as an
additional actor on the side it supported, which in $206$ of the $208$ events
is the side of the Military Forces of Syria. The $132$ events that also
satisfy the strict match provide a more conservative alternative.

\paragraph{Manual review.}
We read the source text of every event that satisfies
equation~\eqref{eq:russia_assign}. Five events are removed because Russia
appears in them only as a landmark (shelling near the Russian embassy in
Damascus) or as a party to a ceasefire agreement. One event is added in which
the reference to Russia appears only in UCDP's location description. After
the review, $208$ events satisfy the assignment rule, $116$ in 2015 and $92$
in 2016.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Airwars records only incidents with reported civilian harm, so the procedure
yields a lower bound on Russian participation. Events outside the Airwars
period, including all events after 2016, cannot be matched and are not
reassigned.
